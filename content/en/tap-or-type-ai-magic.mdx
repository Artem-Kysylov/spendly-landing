## The Classic Form: Built for Muscle Memory

Sometimes, manual input is just faster. If you buy the same coffee every morning, you don't need AI; you need muscle memory.

In the new iOS version, we are leveraging the **Taptic Engine** for satisfying haptic feedback on every tap, making the 3-second logging process feel incredibly solid and rewarding.

## Spendly Pal: The AI Chat for Brain Dumps

Then there are the moments when you are on the go and just want to quickly unload information. Instead of navigating the UI, you just type exactly what happened in natural language.

Our AI core, **Spendly Pal**, handles the cognitive load for you:

> **User:** Spent $45 on groceries, $30 on gas, and $12 for Spotify.
> 
> **Spendly Pal:** Got it! I've added 3 transactions: Groceries ($45), Transport ($30), and Subscriptions ($12).
