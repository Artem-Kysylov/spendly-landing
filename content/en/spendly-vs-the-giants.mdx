## The "Sunday Accountant" Nightmare

Why do giant finance apps insist on zero-based budgeting, 100 sub-categories, and microscopic fonts? This is **over-engineering**. 

When an app requires you to read a manual or watch a 20-minute tutorial just to log a $5 coffee, it has failed you. You are not managing your life; you are performing data entry for a ghost accountant. 

### Why Corporate-Style Trackers Kill Motivation:
* **The Guilt Trip:** If you forget to log expenses for three days, catching up feels like a punishment.
* **Mental Overload:** Choosing between "Food > Groceries" and "Food > Snacks" for a candy bar drains your decision-making energy.
* **The "Desktop" Trap:** These apps are designed for 27-inch monitors, making their mobile versions feel cramped and slow.
